# Governance

## Two parts

- **Ghost Open Standard** (this repository, public): the description of the standard, its principles and its concepts.
- **Ghost Extended Standard** (private): the detailed specifications needed to build compatible devices, gateways, tools and servers. It is shared with licensed manufacturers and partners.

The public description never contains details that could help to attack, clone or duplicate a Ghost system.

## How the standard evolves

- Every change of the standard starts from a recorded decision of the Ghost Eco System team.
- The description here is updated when a concept changes, not for every detail.
- Versions of the detailed specifications are announced to licensed partners.

## Taking part

Manufacturers, integrators and partners who want to build Ghost-compatible products can contact the Ghost Eco System team to obtain a licence and access to the Extended Standard.
